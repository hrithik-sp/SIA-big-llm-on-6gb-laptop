Your name

Sia works for you: it uses your name and follows your instructions and preferences.

Voice

Voice mode (the microphone button) listens and speaks entirely on this laptop. With “Hey Sia”, it ignores other people talking; after it answers you can carry on for a few seconds without saying it again.

Laptop access

Reading: vitals, devices, location, tabs, apps, processes, the clipboard, and any folder or file you ask about. Sia asks before each use and never touches places that hold passwords or keys.

Open apps, files and websites; volume, brightness, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, theme and media; windows; lock, sleep, restart and shut down; screenshots, clipboard, notes and reminders. Each action shows you a card first; sleep, restart and shut down are asked every time. Sia never deletes files, installs software or runs scripts. Every action is logged.
